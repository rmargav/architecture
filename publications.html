Featured Project

A Serene Home That Values Quiet Longevity Over Display

Above the leafy streets of Vadodara, this 2,800 sq ft apartment by Seeking Roots is conceived as a home that values quiet longevity over display. The residence unfolds through a linear plan, with living and open kitchen dining areas placed on either side of a central axis that draws one inward, culminating in a serene temple positioned against an existing window.
The design language is one of restraint, expressed through softened geometry, tactile finishes, and a warm neutral palette. Lime washed walls create a gentle, light-reflecting envelope, while arches, curved ceilings, and moulded details soften transitions between spaces. Italian marble flooring is subtly detailed with grey inlay patterns that guide movement along the linear spine.
Guided by material honesty, mindful reuse, and careful detailing, the apartment reveals a composed, grounded interior that feels quietly lived in and enduring.
