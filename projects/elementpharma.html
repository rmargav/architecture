Client's Brief

A compact 250 sq.ft office designed for efficiency and a refined visual identity.

The brief focused on creating a highly functional office within a compact 250 sq.ft floor area, accommodating a reception, an open workstation for three employees, a pantry and a private Director’s cabin. Crisp whites, veneer textures and a lush green backdrop behind a fluted glass screen bring character to the space, while visual partitions, low-maintenance surfaces and subtle tones ensure an efficient workflow.

Project gallery

Concept Note

Maximizing natural light and creating a compact workspace.

The design makes the most of the natural light and open frontage through an entrance wall of frosted glass set within a metal frame. Colorful murals, rhythmic fluted glass and a carefully detailed metal grid add visual interest, while vertical lines create rhythm and horizontal elements enhance the perception of space. Slender, open furniture profiles further maintain a light and uncluttered feel throughout the office.
