Landscape
Client's Brief

A thoughtfully planned landscape proposal designed to enhance the factory environment through greenery, functionality and a strong visual identity.

The brief for the Luma Optics landscape proposal focused on creating a functional and visually cohesive outdoor environment for the factory building in Vadodara. The design approach considers the relationship between the built structure and its surrounding landscape, using carefully selected planting, materials and spatial elements to enhance the overall character of the site. The scope included conceptual design, design development, material selection and execution, with a focus on creating a landscape that is practical, sustainable and complementary to the architectural setting.

Concept Note

Creating a cohesive landscape that complements the factory architecture while enhancing the site's visual and functional character.

The landscape concept for Luma Optics focuses on creating a balanced relationship between the factory building and its surrounding outdoor spaces. Thoughtful planting, carefully selected materials and structured green areas bring softness to the industrial setting while reinforcing a clean and contemporary visual identity. The design considers circulation, usability and long-term maintenance, creating an outdoor environment that is both practical and visually engaging. Every element is developed to complement the architectural character of the factory and create a welcoming, well-integrated landscape.
