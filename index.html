Our Core Philosophy

About Seeking Roots

Roots are what keep us grounded and bring us back to where we belong. However huge a tree stands, it relies on the strong essence of its roots. A design should bring out the very essence of an individual, group, or organization, and a home should reflect the tastes of its inhabitants. Our inspiration is to retain these roots in terms of shape, material, and color.
The confluence of history with modern style is our foundation. At Seeking Roots, we discover the underlying philosophy, way of living, and purpose behind every building, landscape, and home décor, incorporating it into the design to give you a strong foundation to grow. We provide an innovative outlook to issues of environmental and ecological concern, sustainability, and energy. We enthusiastically seek out projects across varied contexts and cultures to add timeless value combining art with sustainability.
Founder & Principal Architect

LAr. Kinjal Parekh

Kinjal holds an M.Arch in Landscape Architecture from the esteemed CEPT University, Ahmedabad, and completed her B.Arch from SCET, Surat. Her professional journey includes working as a Landscape Architect and Architect with leading firms such as Terra Firma and Oikois in Bangalore, PG Patki Architects in Mumbai, and Essteam in Surat.
Academically, she has been associated as an Assistant Professor with Navrachana University and M.S. University, Vadodara. She is also one of the founders of the Bachelors in Landscape Design program at SEDA, Vadodara.
A Bharatnatyam dancer, national-level Volleyball player, and an ardent traveler, Kinjal aims to bring sustainable yet playful designs to the table at Seeking Roots.
